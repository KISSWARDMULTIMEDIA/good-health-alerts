Good Health Alerts

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

1. Overview

Good Health Alerts ("the app") is a habit and wellness reminder app developed by David Kissward. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

The core promise of the app is simple: your health and habit data never leaves your device. There is no account system, no cloud storage, and no servers run by the developer.

2. Data stored on your device

The following information is stored only in the app's local storage on your device:

  • Your name (used for greetings)
  • Your chosen wake-up and sleep times
  • Your habits and reminders (for example, wake up, water, exercise, meals, medicine)
  • Your daily check-in history, streaks, and achievements
  • Your reminder, tone, dark mode, and other preferences

None of this information is transmitted to the developer or to any third party. You can delete any of it at any time using the “Clear all data” button in the app's Settings.

3. Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device for your chosen reminder times. Notifications are handled entirely on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in your device's system settings.

4. Advertising

The free version of the app displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To display and measure ads, Google or its partners may collect and process device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, and may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and protect against fraud on ads.

By default the app requests non-personalized ads. Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area, the UK, or US states with privacy laws), Google may show you a consent screen before ads are served, and you can change your choices at any time through the privacy options Google provides.

  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google's ads settings and opt-out: adssettings.google.com
  • How Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Health content disclaimer

The tips, reminders, and habits offered by the app are general wellness information only. They are not professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of a qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

7. Security

Because the app stores your data on your device rather than on servers, the data you enter is not transmitted, collected, or shared by the developer. The security of the data on your own device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

8. Your choices

  • Edit or delete any habit at any time.
  • Turn notifications on or off at any time.
  • Clear all app data from Settings, which removes everything stored by the app.
  • Control ad personalization through the consent and privacy options Google provides.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. When it changes, the effective date above will be updated, and the current version will always be available at this address.
